## Acceptance checks for OP, AU and 4D

1. **Clock.** Sample the timeline at t=0, 2.80, 4.10, 5.65, 7.15, 8.00, 8.65, 10.80, 11.35, 11.55, 12.20, 13.75 and 14.00, with frames immediately around the cuts. Check the same pose/overlay at equal visible time at 30, 60 and 120 fps. Simulate skipped frames and a hidden tab. Assert that cues fire at most once and missed lamp pulses never bunch after visibility resumes.
2. **Framing.** Capture the initial pose at 1280×720, 1920×1080 and the smallest supported viewport. Earth's vertical span should be within ±1 px of 31.3 and 46.9 px at the two reference sizes before post-processing, with an unobstructed limb and 56% geometric daylight coverage. Compare an H02 frame taken at the same pose. Earth bearings, phase, visible surface orientation and star layout should match. A player using ordinary walk/look can find the view on their own. Run a cold viewing with the sound off and verify Earth reads on both reveals and the title is first seen in black.
3. **Cue safety.** Count all flashes on captured composites through opening, normal fatal loss and H02, from both a dark room view and the brightest supported console/flight context. Include the alternate reduced-motion capture. Confirm ≤3 flashes in every rolling second, including around the cut and title. In reduced motion, assert no programmed camera displacement, rotation, star distortion, lamp pulses or wake-up nod. Change the motion preference mid-sequence and check that the new mode and the end pose apply cleanly.
4. **Persistence and control.** A pristine Save A opens once and reaches C01 at 14.00 s without input. Try movement, flight keys, ENTER and pointer lock while scripted, then confirm live walking at release. Test reload during black, after handoff, duplicate mount/tab, ordinary loss and Recall, a second fresh save, existing attempt history, completed-save replay/skip and erase. Simulate unavailable persistence and still reach C01. Assert no opening event adds a Black Box attempt or retimes an active simulation.
5. **C01 and H02 integrity.** Compare C01 state, ship clock and initial Black Box/estimator state at handoff with direct seeded entry. Check that ordinary recurrence still resets according to A9 and H02 keeps the exact surviving state and one return record. Check mid-crossing loss, rim contact and centre-of-mass-only crossing separately. Compare loss and H02 presentation events up to reveal under the same preferences.
6. **Audio and release.** Check silent first load, optional user gesture mid-beat, active sound, dropped/missing file, mute, backgrounding, replay skip and handoff. Confirm one room loop, no delayed alarm, and shared failure sound through black for loss/H02. Check that credits carry ElevenLabs and, if used, the exact NASA source and terms. Verify the Pages base path and S15's load/frame budget with generated assets in place. Run `npm run check` and `npm run build` on the integrated code.
