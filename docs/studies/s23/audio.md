AU owns gain and master mute. Keep the dominant spectrum audible on small speakers, tame the alarm relative to the ambient bed and use a short gain ramp at clip stops to avoid digital clicks. De-duplicate the ordinary gameplay alert bus during the authored opening so there is one set of warning sounds. At completion, skip or teardown, stop outstanding one-shots and leave the live room with one ambience loop. If the document hides, suspend the opening cues with its clock.
