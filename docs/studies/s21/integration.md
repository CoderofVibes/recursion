## Reference port contract

`tools/studies/s21/vectors.json` contains the requested **power death**, **informed array run**, **outside gate failure**, and **informed belt thread**. It includes the Cartesian 336-rock fixture, initial ship, law/body parameters, commands, sampled states, reduced electrical loads and expected outcomes. NumPy RNG equivalence is unnecessary; port those states directly. The power-death trajectory stops at its loss timestamp. The outside gate vector is a body-only objective-isolation control; rock survival is deliberately not certified.

Main/lift impulses are **delivered inertial velocity**, not game throttle settings. `robustness.json` supplies the finite-force translation alternative: orient early, use the measured engine scale, spend 71.479 s at calibrated quarter throttle, then correctly oriented RCS for 40.312 s. P6 keeps maneuver choice and execution with the player. Center the finite burns on the supplied impulse epochs. Quarter throttle gives more useful final-tick resolution than a full-throttle one-second pulse.

The vector tolerances apply only to the same physical fixture and commands: same-epoch position 10 m, velocity 0.002 m/s, rock clearance 20 m, sampled gate epoch 120 s, reduced electrical events 60 s and energy 1,000 J. Differences caused by actual attitude/control, thermal restrictions, mode changes, active-function dispatch or different thrust tapes require their own measured envelope; do not loosen a test until it passes or compare different timestamps. The reference's 120/60/30 s orbital convergence and 30/15/7.5 s electrical convergence are retained separately.

## Acceptance tests outside study ownership

1. **Power mechanism:** same depleted fuel with illuminated pointed arrays survives; stowed and deployed edge-on variants lose critical service. Test both batteries empty versus only one isolated bus empty, healthy-spare substitution, latched PDU faults, optional shedding, charge efficiency, ramp startup and temperature-inhibited batteries. Empty reactants alone are not the loss predicate. Assert physical ledgers and no negative inventory.
2. **Timing and recovery:** four-hour caution emits once, drops 2000× to 1×, and appears in ROOM/CONSOLE/FLIGHT/SHIP. Deployment and a deliberate sun hold begun after caution avert loss. A short critical interruption recovers; 120 continuous seconds triggers exactly one loss. Save/reload at 119 s, changes of screen and failed COMMIT do not reset the timer. Simultaneous exit and power failure must resolve chronologically, not award victory from a stale healthy state.
3. **Actual planned and hands-on flights:** run the real ship, controller, recorder and model with both declared power schedules. Request `allDeployed`, perform explicit sun holds between off-sun windows, adapt body-frame RCS, verify real delivered impulse/clearance, use the actual hand input path as well as a committed plan. Measure RCS, heat, cell condition, source margin and archive gaps to exit. The existing 980 km hand-flown example needs retargeting: this cloud proposal makes it unsafe.
4. **Cloud semantics:** no exposure inside the clear support margin; partial deployments and exposed tips; powered/off standard versus power-switch/hardened components; simultaneous spare failures; susceptibility applied once; damage before later interval thrust; trip distinct from irreversible loss. A raw 150-unit homogeneous exposure must accumulate 120 in power-switch parts. Validate the polar cap and wrap at ±180°. Damage may not depend on whether the camera is watching.
5. **Observations and readable blind failures:** first cloud edges acquired before the departure commitment; unknown extent remains unresolved; camera outage removes fresh coverage; stale forecasts widen; no truth leak into screens/model. Reproduce the blind phase-90 rock contact with its same-object warning opportunity, verify tracking actually occurs, and record approach/commands/first failure. Freshly measured +z succeeds; blindly replayed −z can fail. Do not present geometric cues as an already-passed UI test.
6. **Catalogue admission:** every accepted incoming C01 state, complete Alpha+Beta field, exclusion and observation interval, multiple controls, full-hull uncertainty margin, exposure, power, RCS, thermal, wrong-law warning/recovery, and outward gate. Preserve fixed law/body/rock sizes. Reject the old 980 km cloud-contact procedure. Do not multiply the 7-offset, 12-phase and 4-sun-orientation counts into an untested Cartesian product.
7. **Bypasses and optional science:** reproduce the exterior 38.861 m/s failure; search both z signs, earlier/later impulses, split/multiple burns, cloud edges, polar paths and waits beyond 160 h. Re-run with whatever science-burn accounting the owner selects. S8's low-orbit requirement does not fit the existing cumulative cap; never silently raise the cap or discard expenditure. If a legal alternate survives, accept the physical outcome and report the content gap.
8. **Performance and determinism:** identical tapes/JSON restore; all shared boundary tests; S15/A18 profile with observed clouds and populated forecasts, fixed rock-count comparisons, a real modest laptop. Cloud visual overdraw is a separate measurement from particle physics cost.

The generator proves geometry and reduced-resource calculations. The checks above prove the actual game. They remain requests to their owners, not claimed results or reasons to edit outside S21's scope.
