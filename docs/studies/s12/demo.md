# S12 — Demo to capture

**Recommendation [ASSUMED]: use seven slides.** The important evidence is small text: the chosen requirement, what a measurement cost, the forecast's covered interval, and the independent test. Slides let a judge read it. A 30-second cut can convey the loop and the room more vividly, but compresses three attempts and hours of simulated time; it cannot honestly stand for a 30-second learning result. The strongest version of either format ends with a new observation checking a newly issued forecast, after the integrated build is verified.

The limits recorded in [requirements](../../submission/requirements.md) are **at most 30 seconds of video OR seven slides, including title and closing slides**. S12 reopened the [Navigator's answer](https://github.com/nasa/spaceapps/discussions/1947) on 2026-11-14 Pacific; it repeats those limits. The live guide/form and San Marino's format still need confirmation. No deck, video, recording session or upload is delivered by this study: these are production-ready outlines and a capture list.

## Meaning of the two readiness labels

- **shows in the live build now** means the identified view or behavior is evidenced by S9's November 14 live inspection or 2D's recorded browser run. It does not identify the current deployment commit or prove S12 repeated that browser run. Verification/credits cards under this label are editorial cards from current repository evidence, never claimed as an in-game screen.
- **needs 2G's 3D merge** means exclude it from a current-build success claim. The merge is necessary, **not sufficient**: require a clean release build, fresh recorded observations, completed rule test/adoption, and the indicated browser capture. 2G's staged physics checks are not that end-to-end proof.

All actions, timings and cuts below are [ASSUMED] direction. Every public caption has a sentence ID in the [claim ledger](claim-ledger.md), including imperative captions. Keep the visible attempt IDs, ship time, forecast issue time/model version and observation interval where relevant. Ordinary editing can crop/enlarge real footage; it must not draw a successful trace or replace a pending test with a pass.
