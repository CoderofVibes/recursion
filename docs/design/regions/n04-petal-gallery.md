## 3. Experiment, forecast and operation

- **Rival explanations / controlled comparison:** test duplicates, reflections, ordinary occlusion and a paired sightline. Select an apparent common feature, predict its appearance in another petal, then use an unused viewpoint. Keep exposure/pointing comparable. A rejected correspondence is retained and useful; no sacrifice is necessary.
- **Acquisition:** existing optical/attitude sensors and recorder, finite pointing and power. Record rim and feature observations together with uncertainty. Remote views obey S6's visibility rules and the same observation limits as local views.
- **P5 adoption and fit:** the player proposes the connection family; the model fits relative geometry/correspondence parameters from acquired data and tests a held-out view. It cannot invent the topology, label the correct pair from truth or turn a supported view into a proven ship-transfer model.
- **P6 forecast:** predict the player's observation trajectory and apparent feature locations with honest mapping uncertainty. Freeze issued predictions. Far-side mass, range and hazards remain unmeasured unless the observations support them.
- **Operating sequence:** choose hypothesis → reserve sensor/recording load → yaw/pitch toward petal → translate to discriminating view → compare → depart. The procedure is valid only for measured phase, visibility, condition and resource bounds.
- **Readiness / roll:** required views must be reachable with roll held, yaw/pitch and translation. Seeing an upside-down landmark requires no manual ship roll. No viewport-orientation puzzle may require a new control. The ordinary exit depends on clearance, not adoption or visit tokens.

## 4. Consequences and recurrence

- **Outcomes / damage:** supported connection, inconclusive survey, safe bypass/abort, missed view and degraded continuation all count as outcomes. Ordinary collision, thermal overload or exhausted control have physical consequences; simply viewing a petal causes no invented damage.
- **P8 persistence / Black Box:** keep original rim/features, correspondence hypotheses, held-out results, forecasts, commands, configuration and gaps. Loss/Recall restarts the expedition; learned mappings/rule families persist as applicable, but current petal poses need remeasurement.
- **P9 replay:** preserve tested geometry/phase, sensor condition, start state, reserves and model version. Show mismatches and require explicit adaptation; do not automatically retime the observation tour.
- **Repeat experience / tenth run:** skipping the answered gallery is legitimate. Entering it still spends control, sensing and real clearance margin; an old viewpoint can be occluded or physically unsafe under the new seed.
- **Presentation / X boundary:** show observed correspondences with uncertainty, never a truth-colored pairing. S6 must specify paired frame/relative-velocity mapping and clipping at finite rims for the shared X system. No ship transfer or partial ship crossing is an objective here; all petals are too small. Do not invent a different transfer law for H01. If Earth is physically visible through a sensed connection, display and archive it.
