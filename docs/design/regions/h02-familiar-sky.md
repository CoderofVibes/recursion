## 3. What's true underneath

- **It's not a loss.** The Black Box records the run as a **return**: crossing time, surviving state, resources and damage. A9's recurrence doesn't trigger, and the archive, chart and procedures stay as they are. Only the presentation borrows the loss sequence.

- **No truth leaks.** Earth in the window is the view through the window, like every other body. It isn't a label the model hands the player.
- **A loss before the crossing completes is an ordinary loss** (A9: the expedition restarts at C01). There's no checkpoint at H01 or H02.
