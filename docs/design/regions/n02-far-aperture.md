## 3. Experiment, forecast and operation

- **Rival explanations / controlled comparison:** compare a nearby slow object, a distant moving object and occlusion by a foreground limb. The player selects a sideways-and-upward baseline, holds sensor mode comparable and reserves a third view. A baseline parallel to the line of sight may leave ambiguity; that is a useful inconclusive result.
- **Acquisition:** existing optical hardware, attitude sensing and recorder; finite coverage and covariance accompany every fix. Allocate sufficient samples before and after translation; do not manufacture observations when pointing or power fails.
- **P5 adoption and fit:** fit ordinary object states and existing bias parameters. This ordinary parallax task needs no novel rule. A candidate paired connection from N04 is a separate investigation, not an automatic interpretation of this gap.
- **P6 forecast:** show consequences of the player's chosen translation using observed obstacles. Preserve the issued forecast even if later views refute its geometry. Unsensed space and ambiguous tracks remain unsupported.
- **Operating sequence:** choose two target features → reserve observation load → issue forecast → yaw/pitch and translate → coast to measure → check the held-out view → choose departure. Validity depends on baseline, range, angular speed, configuration and actual remaining control authority.
- **Readiness / roll:** fresh depth/velocity estimates and whole-ship clearance support departure; warning quality does not lock the route. Roll is held: change viewing position or yaw/pitch instead of rotating the screen to solve an aperture. Equivalent evidence counts.

## 4. Consequences and recurrence

- **Outcomes / damage:** resolve the ambiguity, transit with a conservative envelope, gather inconclusive evidence, abort, miss a view or continue with degraded sensing. Collision and ordinary electrical/thermal damage follow actual operation; no fictional damage field here.
- **P8 persistence / Black Box:** retain measurements, original correspondences, rejected interpretations, planned/executed burns, configurations, forecasts, gaps and charted leads through loss. Loss/Recall resets the whole expedition; the new seed changes the scene, never the meaning of parallax or learned rules.
- **P9 replay:** saved baselines retain initial geometry, phase, condition, reserves and model version. Disclose mismatches; do not automatically point to the current optimal baseline.
- **Repeat experience / tenth run:** the long lesson can expire, but fresh depth still costs a deliberate viewpoint and samples. Replaying “move left” with a new useful baseline direction wastes propellant and may leave the departure path uncertain.
- **Presentation:** compare two sightline cones and a held-out measured track in 3D; preserve competing hypotheses until supported. No visible portal or transfer mapping is assumed here. Earth observations, if physically available from another region, are not suppressed.
