## 3. Experiment, forecast and operation

- **Rivals / nonverbal lesson:** a power-branch novice sees a sound well forecast but unresolved empty-looking openings; allocating the already available processing channel reveals strands crossing the ship envelope. A sensing-branch novice sees a clear lattice corridor while an outward radial burn bends the measured trail inward. Neither event prints “visit the other branch.” Their frozen forecast, actual trail, channel trace and matched prior observations stay aligned in Black Box; Lantern's other lead is a concrete next-run investigation. A recoverable probe is enough to learn, and the familiar local signal can connect to that lead only if previously observed.
- **Controlled comparison:** short radial/tangential/coast trials outside the lethal corridor distinguish W from calibration; paired channel allocations distinguish unsupported space from clear space. Stop before the measured escape margin is spent. A skilled first-run player may investigate locally and complete without death or visiting both arms.
- **Acquisition / P5:** camera, existing processing hardware, attitude/equipment telemetry and recorder share power. Fit sensed boundary/strand parameters automatically; new rule families require player enablement and independent held-out records. The current model requires a distinct held-out AttemptId, so another segment within the same record does not qualify. The [S4 register](../../studies/S4/README.md) requests non-overlapping same-run experiment records without a physical reset; until implemented, local learning can guide manual flight without validated adoption. Never fabricate an extra attempt from truth or duplicate measurements.
- **P6 / sequence / readiness:** issue a frozen player-authored plan; unresolved layers remain unknown. Track, choose a configuration, enter W with sideways momentum, time burns across its boundary, fold/turn while maintaining channel quality, and clear each full-ship envelope. Measured field extent, control, heat, power and abort reserves determine readiness. The model never chooses or retimes this sequence.

## 4. Consequences and recurrence

- **Outcomes / damage:** success, informative probe, inconclusive reconstruction, safe abort, missed opening alignment, degraded continuation or loss. L exposure accumulates per component, causing persistent electrical deterioration; no two-hit rule or random death. W affects propulsion, so mistiming causes ordinary trajectory/impact consequences.
- **Black Box / P8:** preserve measured first loss of margin, configurations, commands, field samples, sensor gaps, briefing and original forecasts through loss. Loss/Recall restarts C01; records/rules/procedures/chart persist. A one-branch novice usually aborts or fails here, but no mandatory death or knowledge lock enforces that expectation.
- **P9 / tenth run:** fresh phase, reduced heat rejection and consumed RCS invalidate complacent replay even with both rules learned. Match start state, condition/configuration/resources, model, coverage and phase; expose mismatches, do not silently replan.
- **Presentation:** measured 3D ship envelope, uncertainty, trail and per-component trends carry the lesson. Replace the proposal's required roll moment with an off-axis view of a trailing radiator and a yaw/pitch/fold decision. X is not applicable.
