## Open questions and uncertainty

- **Low orbit versus the cumulative cap:** owner/integrator must decide whether low orbit is mandatory or optional evidence gathering. Recommend retaining optional investigation without punishing it through the 16 m/s encounter-wide gate, and enforcing the assist physically. Merely increasing the cap admits stronger direct burns.
- **Wide assists versus A6's belt:** recommend preserving the current belt requirement through a physical crossing witness unless the owner expressly relaxes it. Successful wide assists can miss the dense ring.
- **Seed scope:** recommend finite catalogue plus full-session validation before continuous S4 ranges. This study does not certify arbitrary inherited C01 velocities, ±5% radius offsets, independent orbit inclinations, damaged ship authority, fixed-sun optical/power conditions or whole-game thermal/attitude costs.
