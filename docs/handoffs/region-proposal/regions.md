**Repeat-run pressure and variation:** Keep a recognizable starting landmark, but vary nearby fragments and outgoing bearings. Veterans can perform a short departure check. Clearance permits preparation; current observations and controlled thrust still matter.

**Systems:** REUSE. Existing motion, operation and measurement.
