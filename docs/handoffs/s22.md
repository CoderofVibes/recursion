## Open questions and uncertainty

**First owner decision:** may a very slow physical bypass remain valid? Recommend
yes for the prototype. Finite compact, separated funnels cannot prove a fuel
barrier from arbitrary upstream states. If no, the owner/integrator must define
and separately validate an additional physical transfer or energy constraint.
Do not impose a countdown, fuel-per-distance charge or hidden boundary.

Then decide axial reversal, prototype dimensions/pull strength, gravity-like
IMU invisibility of the pull, precursor/ramp/veto timing, and field/catalogue size
one at a time; [implementation.md](../studies/s22/implementation.md) gives each
recommendation. A19's visible, pulling, crushing direction is already approved.

The study does not establish real camera brightness/parallax, a learned fit,
whole-ship finite turning, resource endurance, N03/P04 continuity, a full-run
reserve margin, or A5/A9 carried-only difficulty. Its seven admitted entries
are **mechanics candidates**. Informed route clearance alone is not playability.
