## Open questions and uncertainty

1. **Install the retrofit on the starting ship?** Yes, conditional on S1/S3 showing a useful optional maneuver. Add the mass explicitly; no equipment appears at encounter entry.
2. **One consumable cassette per run?** Yes. Recover misses, sacrifice a successful release. Multiple swings need a separately budgeted spare/reload design.
3. **Restrict capture to compatible structural rails?** Yes. Grapple geometry/load-path evidence is more defensible than arbitrary harpoon penetration or net wrapping in the minimum version.
4. **Initial simulation depth?** Prove a conservative clear-span scenario with full 3D conservation first; implement flexible contact/wrapping before authoring situations whose consequences depend on it.

Unresolved engineering: S1 must reconcile line compliance/contact/substeps, finite target reactions, material transfer and cut/recoil; S3 must reconcile target scale, measured capture windows, route value and live-time playability with these hardware limits. Low-drag 20–50 m throwing, rail/gate/cutter load capability and the load-frame retrofit are assumed designs awaiting validation. No claimed capture success rate or useful complete route has been demonstrated.

## Notes for the next phase

Estimated integration beyond S1's solver: **2,350–4,100 nonblank TypeScript lines**, plus **1,000–1,800 test lines** `[ASSUMED]`; per-module scope and nine acceptance groups are in the sensing document. These include replay determinism, load/energy/mass conservation, isolated buses, noisy/stale data, carried calibration age, 3D swept clearance and P11 enforcement.

The 4 kW brake rating is not sustainable rejection: at 330 K versus 295 K, its pumped link moves only 875 W. A fixed-point 500 m / 6 kN swing rotates at 0.0198 rad/s, beyond both inherited star tracking and hands-on rate requests (0.01 rad/s). S3 must test lower loads or appropriate moving-target geometry, not silently increase ship authority. Ordinary kit COM changes, launcher recoil and short normal-use fatigue are too small for dramatic gameplay; the consequential constraints are load path, changing COM under depletion, line contact, energy, local heat and evidence quality.
