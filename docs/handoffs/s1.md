## Open questions and uncertainty

One owner decision each, through the integrator; full tradeoffs in `docs/studies/s1/integration.md`:

1. **Proceed with the optional Borrowed Spin encounter after 2G?** Recommend yes, conditional on S2 capture/load-path margins and S3/3E encounter verification.
2. **Allow arbitrary cable entanglement in the first version?** Recommend no: one line with known straight/authored-guide topology first. The best version adds segmented finite-mass cable and swept contact; the smaller one loses emergent draping, wraps and recoil/whip.
3. **Change structure inertia between runs?** Recommend no: keep mass, geometry and material laws fixed; vary measured phase/orientation/approach under A10. The older region proposal's inertia variation should not silently override that direction.
4. **Does every cable break automatically kill the ship?** Recommend no: apply actual mount damage, release trajectory and collision consequences; permit a physically valid abort.

Important boundaries: no hook/contact/deployment simulation, line/hardware mass, arbitrary wrap formation, self-contact, friction, distributed gravity torque, finite-element flexibility or medical qualification. `slack_wrap` genuinely routes through a fixed frictionless guide, but its topology is supplied at capture. Release exports stored elastic energy without modeling a whip or recoil impulse. Commands use integer ticks; arbitrary float scheduling is rejected. Threshold search assumes the resolved microstep has no unseen interior spike; fixture refinement supports it only at the tested scale.
