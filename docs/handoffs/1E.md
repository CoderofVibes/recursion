## Round 4 — Part 2: instrument primitives, 3D materials, UI lab (2026-11-14, ~14:20 Pacific)

### Delivered
- **`src/theme/components/`** — React primitives with no game logic. They take plain numbers and strings, a `status` (nominal, caution, warning, off, stale) and a `tone` (measured, forecast, blackbox).
  - `LevelBar`: a level against a scale, with limit, caution, warning and target marks and an optional forecast tick. It is a `meter` for assistive technology.
  - `Trend`: measured data as a solid trace; an optional forecast continuing it as a dotted teal line inside its 2σ band after a rule at now; an optional earlier attempt in orange; dashed limit rules.
  - `Readout` and `ReadoutGrid`: label over value, with unit, optional ± sigma, four sizes.
  - `Schematic` and `SchematicBox`: boxes on a grid joined by right-angled links; buses drawn heavier; off paths dashed; boxes selectable.
