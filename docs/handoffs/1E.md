### The owner's direction (verbatim; request: add to `docs/CREATIVE_BRIEF.md`)

> My vision is a real 3D room with a large center command station console in the middle. When the player "revives" they enter the room and have basic wsad controls to walk. There is a large panoramic (maybe 90-180 degree) viewing window of the expanse before the player and the control console. The console can do many things. Click on map and a large holographic 3D map projects. It is interactive - get the full view of the whole universe the player is within, or select sections, actual encounter environments - I'd like information pertinent to the environments to be available in this map.
>
> Note - when the map is selected, the player view goes to the map. The whole room is no longer in view so the player can focus on the visuals and interaction with the map. I'd like to know what other features of this map you'd think we should add, given the context you have regarding the game.
>
> There should also be a ship view that lets the player inspect the ship, select different systems, everything you can think of there - in 3D holographic view. Please let me know if you have questions from other threads for details needed for any of this. Again, when the player selects this, the view is on this 3D ship selection and the whole room is no longer visible so the player can focus.
>
> The main console can be accesses which is where all of the other functions and screens can be accessed. Anything from data assessment, predictive model viewing, system conditions and summary, Black Box (whatever we decide on that meaning) basically everything else. While the map and ship view are 3D and projected above the main console, this screen is in the console so the player is looking down into it. The screen on the console takes over the screen the player sees.
>
> There is also a flight screen the player will enter during encounters. I'd like for it to be a 3D projection above the console like the other 3D projections, but in this you can see the back ground which is the view of the environment ahead. The player should have the holographic ship in front of them, with perhaps things like a center of mass dotted line, the ship's movement and tilt/rotation on an access, controls for thrust forward/back, and lateral movement. Objects and other scans (think the lattice when the player makes the engineering decision to allocate resources to the black box scanning function) will pop up on this 3D display and the window in the back drop should also show if an object is approaching (like a big asteroid - it should be seen through the window and on the 3D projection).
>
> When the ship is destroyed and the player fails the screen should go black (maybe some camera shaking up and down to simulate the space craft's failure) and the game restarts at the beginning (roguelikes are cruel) with the player walking into the control room and seeing the initial environment. Player hands and such are not part of this game - just a 1st person view and a small white dot on the screen to show what the player is interacting with (something subtle like that).

### Delivered
- `prototypes/room/`: a walkable mock-up of that brief in the Night Watch theme (standalone, Three.js 0.170.0 from jsDelivr, served by the same `serve.mjs` at `/room/`).
  - **Room:** first-person walk (WASD, mouse look), a panoramic window whose arc is adjustable from 90° to 180°, the central console with four lit pads (MAP, SHIP, CONSOLE, FLIGHT), its screen set into the top, a projector ring, and Black Box as a physical orange recorder docked in the console's end. A small white dot is the only HUD; it opens into a ring and names what it has found.
  - **Map:** the camera goes to the hologram and the room goes dark. Charted regions with a "surveyed" arc, corridors flown, leads drawn as bearing fans (a lead is a direction until a second bearing crosses it), earlier attempts from Black Box with ✕ loss and ↺ recall marks, a reach ring for the propellant on board, unsurveyed space hatched. Selecting a region shows a plate (open question, evidence held, model state, hazards, procedure, transfer forecast) and draws the transfer as a forecast; REGION VIEW zooms into the local environment as sensed.
  - **Ship:** the vehicle from `data/ship-baseline.json` (all 42 assemblies at their baseline positions, the four deployment configurations and footprints), selectable, with system layers (power, thermal, propulsion, sensing, data, life), relationship lines, centre of mass with its dotted yaw axis, the main engine's line of action and thrust arm, and a part plate (modes, bus, loop, draw, heat, mass, spare, history, condition).
  - **Console:** the camera looks straight down into the console and its screen takes over the view: placeholder pages for systems, power, thermal, model, data, Black Box and plan. Clicking the Black Box recorder opens its page.
  - **Flight:** the local environment as a hologram above the console with the window behind it. A large holographic ship with centre of mass, dotted yaw axis, bearing ring and yaw-rate arc, velocity, thrust chevrons at the points of application; measured trail, issued forecast with envelope and horizon; a tracked rock with closest-approach alert; W/S/A/D/Q/E thrust keys shown on screen; BLACK BOX SCAN at three power levels brings up the lattice reconstruction.
  - **Loss:** red frame, about 1.5 s of vertical shake and flicker, cut to black, one orange pip and the next attempt number, then the player is back at the door. The map keeps the lost attempt's track.

### Open questions and uncertainty (owner decides unless noted)
1. **Flying:** hands on the thrusters in real time, a plan of timed burns that the player commits and watches, or both? The build plan has a plan editor and warp (2D), not live thrust keys.
2. **Time:** does the world keep moving while the player walks the room or studies the map or ship, or only in the flight view? The brief (§10) asks for safe preparation without a universal countdown.
3. **Planar flight (P1) with a window:** everything outside sits on one line across the window, like a belt seen edge-on. Accept it, or reopen P1? Reopening it is a large change to 2A.
4. **The map and the brief:** the brief says the exit is not labelled and leads do not say what they are. The mock-up therefore shows the universe as charted so far, not the whole authored expedition. Confirm.
5. **Room scale:** larger than the hull allows (as mocked), or true to the 5 m hull and cosier?
6. **Window arc:** the mock-up defaults to 150°.
7. Flight hologram: heading-up or chart-up by default.
8. Is the loss sequence right in length and severity (about 6 s from impact to walking again)?
