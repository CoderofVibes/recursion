## Update 2 at a glance

On the integrator's instruction, after merging `main` (2C merge, 1G revision 2, its contracts): two pieces, in this order.

1. **Asteroid tracks (spec §10, DECISIONS A6).** A tracked asteroid becomes a test-particle arc: each of its bearing-and-range fixes, combined with the ship's path from the ship's own Tier L window fits, becomes a fix of each body as seen from the asteroid. Its windows join Tier L at its own distances, so the calibration profile and the strength-against-distance plot cover distances the ship never flew. Forecasts carry each tracked asteroid's track (`objects`) and its closest approach to the ship (`closestApproaches`).
2. **1G revision 2 (DECISIONS A5).**
   - Two-part hedge: earned σ_e inside an adopted rule's validated spans, default σ_h0 elsewhere. `ForecastInputs.hedge` is now σ_h0; `earnedHedge` holds σ_e and its spans per body.
   - Validated spans: one per body per passing test, in `ExtensionState.validatedSpans`. The transfer is shared-law (A5 "every body").
   - Aging of ship calibrations. Each attempt fits an arrival value plus its own deviation, with a prior σ from the aging that attempt accrued. `ParameterEstimate.arc` and `freshness` are set. A forecast's engine terms (`ForecastInputs.engine`) are aged to the time of issue. `carriedCalibration` ages the carried values by the transit.
   - `ForecastSample.validated` and `ModelState.noiseScale` are set. `REVISION_2_PENDING` is deleted and the guard covers all **74 claims**.

### Open questions and uncertainty (update 2)

- **2F: declare asteroids as known-size targets** if the Gravity range should let players probe the law with them (A6); without a range their fixes are not used.
- **Asteroid probes in Tier G and in rule tests.** Probes feed Tier L and forecasts only. They are built from Tier L's ship path, so they do not enter Tier G. A rule's validated span therefore covers the ship's distances, not the asteroids'. Feeding probes into rule tests would let asteroid data widen a validated span, which is a design choice for 1G or the owner.
- **A deviation split at a mid-arc damage event** (spec §3.7, not vector-tested) is not built: an arc with a condition drop gets one deviation with the drop in its prior.
- **Multiple bodies:** probe arcs use frozen bodies from the ship arc's solution; only the single-body case is tested.

### Open questions and uncertainty

- **Asteroids are absent from forecasts.** *(Done in update 2.)* The model tracks massive bodies only, so `objects` and `closestApproaches` cover bodies, not asteroids. 1G specified asteroid tracking as an option (spec §10) without vectors. The brief's "predicted movement of nearby objects" and Gravity-range clearance will want it: fit each tracked asteroid as a test particle from its fixes and the ship's believed path, then propagate it under the believed law. 2F decides whether the first playable needs it.
- **Specified by 1G, not built:** unseen-body mode, region thrust response (Inverse Wells, Phase 3), outlier flags, sigma-point forecasts, pointing terms.
- **Several bodies at once:** a second body's windows and the present state use the other bodies frozen at their Tier G solution, as designed. The empirical-acceleration report with more than one modelled body freezes the other bodies from the first arc's solution, which is approximate for the other arcs. Only the single-body paths and the seen-body extension are vector-tested.
- **Sensor noise** in the vectors is 1G's placeholder. The model trusts each measurement's reported σ and widens envelopes by the noise scale s² when the data say σ is too small; retuning belongs to balance (2F, 5A).
- **Determinism** is bit-exact on one JavaScript engine (lib README); rebuild = live was shown in Node and the browser runs the same code.
