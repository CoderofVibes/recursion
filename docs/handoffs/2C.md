### R27 answer (for 3A, through the owner)

Consumed, with one problem at the source that needs 3A's fix. **Nothing leaks today, but it will as soon as reported and true condition can differ.**
- The contract defines `condition-changed.condition` as the **reported** condition. The sensors and the `condition` telemetry use `values.reportedCondition ?? condition`.
- 3A's emitters push the equipment's own `condition`, which is the true state: `src/ship/condition/index.ts` (`events.push({ kind: 'condition-changed', …, condition })`) and `src/ship/thermal/protection.ts` (the same).
- Today nothing outside tests sets `values.reportedCondition`, so the two are equal and nothing true reaches the record.
- The fix: emit `values.reportedCondition ?? condition`, and emit on a change of that value. Without it, true damage would reach the record, the Black Box archive and the model's aging. The model and Black Box cannot tell the difference from the event alone.

**A question for the integrator:** `exposure` events carry each component's absorbed dose (`amount`). The contract lists them as something the ship noticed, and the Black Box records them. If the ship has no instrument that measures per-component dose, that is true state in the archive. 2C reads them nowhere.

## Update 5 (2026-11-15): the lattice from recorded data (3C R9, S17), redundant model work (S20), R22

Merged `main` at `00b3b9f` (a fast-forward). Commits: `6e76c58` (reconstruction) and one for the Black Box function, the client queue and the docs.

### What changed

1. **The lattice is rebuilt from recorded angular frames** (`src/model/lattice/`; 3C R9; DECISIONS A16, P11, P13; S17 `docs/studies/s17/black-box.md`).
   - **Decoding (`frames.ts`).** A strict decoder of 3C's `lattice.angular-fragments.v2` layout, with 3C's own validation: malformed or unknown frames are rejected, never guessed. Frames are taken in canonical acquisition order, and a product counts only once delivered.
   - **Poses (`poses.ts`).** The camera pose is taken at **acquisition**, not at product delivery, from records only:
     - attitude from the star tracker;
     - position from the ship's believed path: the arc's solution where the model has one, otherwise the arrival estimate carried forward with the executed thrust;
     - position and velocity covariance, never smaller than the arrival survey's.
   - **Fit (`reconstruct.ts`).** In five steps:
     1. Coincident fragment endpoints in each frame become one node.
     2. Nodes are linked across frames only when the match is mutual and unambiguous (ratio test). An ambiguous node starts a new track, so ambiguity stays unresolved.
     3. Each point is triangulated on its earlier observations, then all points are fitted jointly with the pass's pose offset and drift (bundle adjustment). With no drift prior this is exactly S17's fit.
     4. Each point is held-out-tested on its later observations.
     5. Strands are drawn only from observed connected support.
   - **Coverage** is `unresolved` wherever the camera looked and absent elsewhere. Nothing is ever `clear-supported`.
   - **Versioning.** Each result carries `LATTICE_RECONSTRUCTION_VERSION` (1) and a hash of its settings.
2. **The Black Box function (A16).**
   - `ModelService.lattice(attemptId?, now?)` and `ModelClient.lattice` return `BelievedField[]`, with `visual` in 3C's `lattice-observations-v1` schema, for `BelievedWorld.fields`. Each result is named by its own run's `frameId` and `attemptId`, so an earlier run is an archive view, never the current map. `latticeDetail` gives the full reconstruction.
   - `src/blackbox/lattice.ts` accounts for retention from the record alone:
     - frames, fragments and their bytes;
     - sequences never retained;
     - truncated frames;
     - the latest raw tile, with its age measured from acquisition;
     - when the function was switched on and off.
3. **S20: redundant model work.** The client runs calls one at a time in order. A waiting `refresh` or `refit`, with only `append`s queued after it, is superseded by a later one, and its caller gets the later result. Records are never dropped, and nothing crosses a forecast, a load or a rule decision. `busy()` reports what is running, since when, how many calls are queued and how many were superseded. After `terminate`, waiting calls are refused.

### Open questions and limits (update 5)

### What changed

1. **The modelled bodies' tracks carry real uncertainty (2F request 13).** The model moves the bodies about each other under its own law. That law has not been sampled at their separation (4 r₀ in the Gravity range), and the resulting error used to sit in no uncertainty source.
   - `bodiesAt` (engine) gives each other body's state relative to the forecast's reference body, with what the model knows of it: the fit's covariance (× s²), or the briefing's carried forward, and how the hedge has moved it since the pair was last fixed.
   - The hedge now acts on the bodies' mutual pulls (`Structure.bodyProfiles`). Only the hedge factor applies, so the mean is unchanged.
   - In `forecastCore` each other body's state is six uncertain sources (`othersCov`), coupled to the same hedge exponents as the ship (`othersEta`). It also returns `bodyCov` and `relCov`.
   - Tracks carry those covariances. The horizon and the closest approaches use the covariance relative to each body.
   - Every covariance in a forecast is relative to `detail.presentBody`, whose own track carries none.
2. **Two moving bodies, with 2F requests 2 and 12, on the 3D model.**
   - *Request 12.* The present state is fitted on whichever body the camera fixed most recently, and the forecast runs relative to that body. `detail.presentBody`, `presentAge` and `stale` (older than `settings.presentStaleAfter`, 3,600 s) say which body it was and how old the state is.
   - *Request 2.* `lawInForce` supplies the exponent, once its rule is adopted, and the masses in force. These are used to carry the briefing's bodies forward: for Tier G's per-arc priors in flight, and as the fallback for a body without a solution.
   - *The present-state fit now carries the other bodies.* Their states are prior-held parameters. The hedge exponents and the unprofiled masses are consider parameters: the fit is unchanged, `Anchor.hedge` gives the error per unit of each source, and the forecast couples it (`xNowEta`). Without this, a misplaced Beta biased the present state near Alpha by up to 4.9σ.
   - *New guard.* A converged global fit is not believed for a body no arc fixed when it moves that body's mass or state beyond 3σ of its prior (`settings.bodyPriorPullLimit`). The mass is then held at its prior, with the prior's σ, and the body comes from the briefing.
3. **A18 (1): a forecast from the moment of commitment while time keeps running.** `buildForecast` works as of `issuedAt`: it uses the record up to then (`recordAsOf`) and a present state from those data only. It never counts the forecast plan's own executed commands (matched by `planId`).
4. **S8's checks (`src/model/s8.test.ts`).** S8's states, carried by this integrator, match S8's own propagation within its tolerances. The model then sees only what the ship records. S8's checks found two failures, both now fixed:
   - The Newtonian global fit put Beta's μ at 1.4e10 (prior 2.2e9 ± 8e8) to absorb a dive's law error. Forecasts became up to 13σ wrong. Fixed by the guard above.
   - Between the bodies no rock could be forecast: the 20% rule (spec §13.7) dropped every window there, and those windows are the only source of the ship's path that a rock's fixes are referred to. Those windows are now fitted for the path (not the profile) in attempts that tracked rocks.
5. **S15 rank 5: the in-thread fallback is lazy-loaded.** `client.ts` imports `service.ts` on the fallback's first call, and runs calls in order behind `init`. `index.ts` now exports values only from the light modules (`client.ts`, the new `context.ts` with `contextFromBriefing` and `contextFor`, `settings.ts`) and everything else as types.

## Update 3 (2026-11-14 13:58 PST): runs, decisions, asteroids in rule tests

Three follow-ups the integrator asked for after merging update 2 (906370f), done after `git merge main` (a fast-forward).

1. **Each run starts from different geometry (A10).** When an attempt records its briefing (`AttemptStart.briefing`), that briefing's bodies, asteroids and arrival estimate replace the encounter's for that attempt (`contextFor(ctx, record)` in `engine.ts`). That covers the priors on its second body's state per arc, the arrival present state, the fallback for frozen bodies in Tier L, and its forecasts' frame and clearances. Priors, masses and the ship still come from the encounter context.
2. **Adopt and revoke are recorded** in `BlackBoxArchive.extensionDecisions`, and a rule's status comes from its last decision, so a revoked rule stays revoked after a reload.
   - `extensions.ts`: `decide`, `adoptedFrom`, `decisionsFromAdopted` (older saves), and `extensionStates(tests, decisions)`. The old `adoptExtension` and `revokeExtension` are gone.
   - Service and client: `adopt(ext, at?)`, `revoke(ext, at?)`, `decisions()`, and `load({ …, decisions })`.
   - Black Box: `withExtensionDecision` (keeps `adoptedExtensions` as the result) and `extensionDecisions` in the store's meta record.
3. **Asteroid fixes count toward a validated span (A5).** A rule test now fits the tracked asteroids of its training and held-out attempts along with the ship. The held-out test takes several arcs (`heldOutConsistency` accepts an arc list), and `tierGProblem` accepts probe arcs. The passing test's span around each body covers the asteroid fixes measured relative to that body. σ_e still comes from the ship's held-out windows.

Two bugs were found and fixed on the way:

- **Ship path across attempts.** With several attempts, the asteroid fixes took the ship's path from a window of whichever attempt started at a similar time. `probes.test.ts` had only one attempt, so it couldn't show this; the new span test did. The path is now taken from the asteroid's own attempt and ship arc.
- **Briefed bodies treated as asteroids.** A briefed body the model did not yet include (B, before `unmodeled-body:B`) was handled as an asteroid probe. Probes now exclude every briefed body.
