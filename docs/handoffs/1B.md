## Claims, labelled

- [FICTIONAL] P4 is dimensionally consistent with K = GM sqrt(r0), in m^3.5/s². Recommend **one shared, fixed r0** for all bodies/pairs; otherwise ordinary reciprocal forces and inertial mass ratios are not assured.
- [ESTABLISHED: R03] The general near-circular central-force method supplies the apsidal limit. [FICTIONAL] Applied to p = 2.5, the prograde advance is **149.116882° per radial cycle**, not per 360° revolution; escape/circular speed is **1.154700538**.
- [FICTIONAL] Circular-binary triangular equilibria require q(1−q) < (3−p)² / [3(p+1)²] for strict planar linear spectral stability. At p = 2.5, qcrit ≈ 0.00684964. All five equilibria of the proposed q = 0.2 binary are unstable. No stable parking point or asteroid belt is established.
- [FICTIONAL] A Newtonian fit infers GM sqrt(r0/r), but a single-radius circular orbit cannot identify the exponent. Compare different ranges and held-out coasting arcs; primary-relative tracking must include the secondary's acceleration of the primary.
- [ASSUMED] Candidate scale: r0 = 1e6 m, primary/secondary GM = 1e10 / 2.5e9 m³/s², separation 4e6 m, collision radii 250/160 km. Gravity uses point centers; the inverse-square shell theorem does not justify exact extended-body fields here.
- [ASSUMED] Candidate ship only: 30 t initial mass, 4 t usable propellant, 20 kN, 320 s Isp gives about 449 m/s ideal total delta-v; typical 20–80 m/s burns last about 30–118 s. 1C owns the actual baseline.
- [ASSUMED] Use 1 s fixed-step velocity Verlet as a starting numerical proposal, with horizon-specific convergence checks and declared finer subdivision where needed. Warp changes tick count, not time step.
- [FICTIONAL] Inverse Wells reflect only the radial **thrust acceleration**, consume ordinary fuel and leave coasting velocity continuous. The norm-bounded blend prevents thrust amplification but deliberately exchanges momentum/energy with an unmodeled field.
- [ASSUMED] A planar blind-traversal illustration uses one transverse clear-width fraction per independent layer. With 1 km spacing, 200 m effective severe margin and 12 layers, avoidance of every severe interval is about 0.218%. Actual survival requires authored geometry, speed, extended-component dose integration and damage thresholds.
