## Open questions and uncertainty

- **Owner: the objective.** Proposed: the low-orbit band. Alternatives: a moving gap, a rendezvous (both need asteroid tracking and forecasting in the model). The band, hold and reserve numbers are play values.
- **Owner: the sun** (P14 item 3): 600 W/m², direction 200°.
- **Propellant.** A good run spends about 22 m/s of the 690 m/s main load, so main propellant is not scarce here. RCS is: a 180° turn costs 15–29 kg of 300 kg (see request 2).
- **Playability is not shown.** The ten-minute criterion needs 2C's model, the console and a player.
- **Noise replays after recurrence.** The arrival world includes the sensor noise streams, so two identical flights give identical measurements. That keeps replays exact (P2, P9), but an estimator must not count such data twice. Integrator and 2C decide whether noise should differ per attempt.
- **Range noise.** 1C's camera ranges to 5% (50 km at r₀); 1G's vectors assumed 0.2%. Bearings carry the navigation. Balance question for 2C, 5A.
- **Loss causes.** Power, thermal and propellant exhaustion do not end an attempt yet.
- **Warp near burns.** At 1000× a burn passes in a blink. An automatic slow-down is not built.

## Requests outside my ownership

1. **2A / integrator, `src/sim/simulation.ts`:** `Work.store()` rebuilds the world without `sun`, so it is gone after the first `step` or `run`. Add `...(prev.sun ? { sun: prev.sun } : {})`. 2F puts it back each tick meanwhile (`truth-step.ts`).
2. **2B → 3A, attitude control:** holding an inertial attitude costs about 12 kg/h of RCS with nothing disturbing the ship (no deadband; minimum-impulse pulses limit-cycle), and a 180° turn costs 15–29 kg and peaks at 0.18 rad/s, 18 times the star trackers' limit. Please add an attitude and rate deadband and a slew-rate limit. Until then `burnSteps` releases the attitude after each burn.
3. **2B → 3A / 3C, speed:** `stepShipSystems` takes about 0.23 ms per tick, which caps warp near 2,000×. Mass properties and geometry are recomputed several times a step.
4. **Integrator, `src/contracts/truth/encounter.ts`:** give `EncounterDefinition` an arrival procedure (`arrivalCommands: Command[]`) and truth-side objective rules, or adopt `EncounterSetup` (`src/game/encounter.ts`).

6. **Integrator, `src/contracts/encounter.ts`:** add the P4 reference radius to `EncounterBriefing`, so the model does not take it from content.
