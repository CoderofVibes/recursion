### R20: sensors within the tick — measured, and not done (report)

- **The Cathedral's frames are not a tick artefact.** At 350 W (Survey) the lattice processor's update rate is 0.2 Hz by design (`latticeProcessing.minUpdateRate`; 2 Hz at 1,000 W). Survey from arrival records frames at 30.2, 35.1, 40.1, 45.2, 50.1 and 55.1 s: one every 5 s after the camera's warm-up, each retained in the first recorder packet after it is acquired. The Cathedral steps at 0.1 s, and its 10 Hz recorder, 10 Hz monitor and every frame deadline fall on tick starts. Acquisition already happens at the real state, within 0.1 s of its due time. Sub-tick scheduling would change nothing there.
- **In the 1 s regions it would multiply the record.** The recorder's normal rate is 10 Hz and the IMU's 50 Hz. Acquiring at each instrument's own time would mean ten recorder samples per tick in the Gravity range and the Well Garden: ten times the Black Box record, the hand-over to the model and its storage, at the cost of the learning loop NIGHT just unblocked. The ship already reports this cap honestly (`black-box.sample-rate` is min(rate, 1/dt)). So I left it. If the owner wants it anyway, it is a split of the ship step at sensor deadlines, the same mechanism as 3A's conditioned pieces.
