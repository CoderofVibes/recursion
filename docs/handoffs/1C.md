## Open questions and uncertainty
- Owner approval is still needed at the Phase 1 gate for this proposed architecture and ratings. No approval is implied by committing the proposal.
- Illumination in the unfamiliar universe remains unknown. The default zero solar allocation is conservative; actual measured irradiance can enable generation. The owner/integrator should decide content inputs without deriving illumination from the changed gravity law.
- Retain optional NEXT-scale ion propulsion only if approved expedition timescales make long coasts useful. No short-maneuver thrust boost is proposed.
- The structural mass allowance, lumped thermal capacities, local noise/sigma assumptions and compact-component exposure sampling need implementation/playtest refinement. The checker verifies static balances, not a stable regulator under every transient or a route through an encounter.
- Baseline RCS banks share pod temperatures in the initial stub. Independent dormant-nozzle cold soak needs explicit local thermal states and heater-energy routing in Phase 3; do not claim that behavior from the simple shared node.
