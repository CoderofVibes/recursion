## Open questions and uncertainty

- Reference accuracy is ~1 cm, limited by GMAT's sub-microsecond stop timing at the
  first propagation (epoch resolution), not its integrator. Ample for the game;
  don't quote the vectors as more precise.
- One build on one platform. Other GMAT builds may differ by millimetres.
- GMAT's GUI Help/About was not opened; the release name comes from the package
  `README.txt` and the zip name, the build date from the console and logs.
- The 0.05 m tolerance is a recommendation; the integrator/2A decide.
