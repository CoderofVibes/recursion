## Claims, labelled

- [FICTIONAL] Lattice units, susceptibility, dose buckets and burnout are the existing
  baseline game rules, not radiation dosimetry. Susceptibility applies once to dose;
  burnout uses strict raw intensity thresholds 8/10/20 U.
- [DEMONSTRATED: condition tests] Nonlinear prefix-dose localization finds 0.1 s
  burnout within 0.25 ms; off-state buckets do not fire a backlog; reset retains dose
  and permanent condition; JSON retains contiguous dwell.
- [DEMONSTRATED: power/service.test.ts] Source loss redispatches downstream service;
  a selected healthy spare counts, standby does not; a dual trip differs from dual
  permanent PDU loss. Empty fuel with usable solar is not loss.
- [DEMONSTRATED: condition/s17.test.ts] Independent compact-mount polynomial
  exposure with actual dispatch/heat matches S17's camera burnout at
  7.8022441859 s and both PDUs at 11.3457340755 s within 1 ms. This qualifies those
  components, not the full 42-part first-contact geometry.
- [DEMONSTRATED: thermal/transit.test.ts] At 600 W/m², arrays and radiators fully
  deployed, quiet payloads and free attitude, 72 h consumes no fuel-cell reactants
  or RCS propellant. Both packs reach 36 MJ. Final cabin/service are 295.00 K,
  external loop 287.25 K and exchanger 292.66 K; all nodes change <0.02 K between
  hours 48 and 72, no equipment trips/damage and no unmet power. Maximum thermal
  ledger residual is 5.49e-6 J per 10 s step. This uses finite real hardware and
  normal life-support loads; it is not a certificate for every route/attitude.
- [ASSUMED] Radiation degradation linearly reduces remaining condition while
  preserving unrelated damage; baseline leakage adds real electrical draw/heat.
  Battery capacity loss dissipates rejected stored charge as local heat once.
- [DEMONSTRATED: thermal/depth.test.ts; config/faults.test.ts] Cold heaters can
  recover intact hardware; reset cannot repair frozen/overheated hardware;
  a common-loop leak defeats either pump; a jam preserves intermediate geometry
  shared by clearance, inertia and cooling. No repair/refill exemption is added.
