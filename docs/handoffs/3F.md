## Delivered

**The console as the loop** (items 1, 3, 6; `src/console/`)
- `ConsoleScreen.tsx`, `stages.ts`, `instruments/LoopRail.tsx`: the rail. EXPLORATION, SYSTEMS, PLAN, FLIGHT, BLACK BOX, MODEL, numbered 1 to 6; each a button whose second line is a fact about the game ("ends T+04:43:00 · beyond the data", "attempt 1 · off by 3.2σ", "slope −0.50 · the law does not fit"). FLIGHT goes to the hologram. CREDITS is a quiet button in the footer.
- `pages/ExplorationPage.tsx`: the region as the ship holds it (bodies, rocks, where each pull is measured, the path flown, the forecast, the objective's place), the objective as bars, what Black Box holds here, the attempt's design, ENTER.
- `pages/SystemsPage.tsx`, `power.ts`: supply against demand on one board; faders for the loads the player sets (camera, science processor, Black Box rate, comms) with their modes as stops; what each setting buys (endurance against the plan, fixes a second and what limits them, heat, buses); stores and configuration; every unit, folded. **A setting made before ENTER shows at once as set and is sent on ENTER** (`desk.setUnit`, `desk.enter`): before, nothing set in preparation showed until the ship ran.
- `pages/PlanPage.tsx`: the forecast of the plan as the lead chart, with its steps to walk, what the forecast says at the step shown and what its uncertainty is made of. **The forecast follows the plan as it is edited** (the forecast of the plan as it was stays, faint, until the new one arrives). A burn's settings are bars to pull and figures to type; its direction is a chip. PREVIEW, COMMIT and CUTOFF sit under the chart, always in view. Procedures and RECALL fold below.
- `pages/BlackBoxPage.tsx`, `regionChart.ts`: **predicted against flown is the lead**: the flight as its record measured it, drawn over the forecast issued for it, seen from the body the ship ranged; where it left the forecast is marked; the attempt before is behind it; a stretch with no fix is left undrawn. Beside it the verdict (held or left, when, the worst miss) and how far off the forecast the flight ran over time; then the debrief and, for a loss, the loss review. Attempts, Compare (with SET FLIGHT OVERLAY) and RECALL on the left; the recorder's store and ERASE RECORDS fold.
- `pages/ModelPage.tsx`, `StrengthPlot.tsx`: measured strength against distance for each body the model has measured, with the trend the model reports drawn beside its law; after a rule is adopted the same measurements from before stay beside the new picture. The rule's test as its three steps (fit on, check on, result), the held-out statistic and the misfit with and without the rule as bars. Parameters, calibration and confounding, CHECK BURNS and FIND UNEXPLAINED PULL fold.
- Every capability of the old console is kept: burn and pointing planning; PREVIEW, COMMIT, CUTOFF; procedures (save, replay, forget) and RECALL / BEGIN AGAIN; records, debrief, compare, overlay, erase; TEST, ADOPT, REVOKE; systems and configuration; the encounter panels (untouched).

**Where a plan has to get to (R51)**
- The objectives are on the charts (`regionChart.ts` `objectiveRegions`, `TrajectoryChart` `regions`): a place to reach is a dashed ring where it is; PLAN's own framing points to one it does not hold, at the chart's edge, with the distance from the ship; WHOLE REGION holds it.
- PLAN reads the forecast against the objective (`forecastReach`, `data.ts` `departForecast`): how near it comes to each place to reach, or when it reaches it; for a departure, the speed and distance from the centre of mass where the forecast ends, and whether the ship is heading in.
- A burn can **STEER**: across the ship's motion, on the side of a body or a place to reach (`steerToward`). TOWARD and AWAY take places to reach too. Also across the motion to the LEFT, RIGHT, UP or DOWN (`acrossMotion`).
- 2F's second note (the duplicate "Past the forecast horizon") was already handled in `alerts.ts`: the console's own advisory stands down when the session raises `forecast-ended`. The rail raises PLAN, not SYSTEMS, for it.
