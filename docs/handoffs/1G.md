Thread: Claude (launched by the owner in place of GPT/Codex; Opus 5.5 for the first pass, Fable 5.2 for review, completion and revision 2) · Branch: `p1/1g-model-spec` · Date/time (Pacific): first handoff 2026-11-14 11:40 PST; **revision 2 (DECISIONS A5) 2026-11-14 12:35 PST**

## Revision 2 at a glance (for 2C and the integrator)

Owner direction A5: carried knowledge must help without making later encounters easy. Two additions, both specified and vector-tested.

1. **Validated range (spec §7.4, §8.2).** A rule's earned hedge now carries the distance span its passing test covered and applies only inside it. The forecast hedge has two parts: earned inside validated spans, default elsewhere.
2. **Parameter aging (spec §3.7).** Ship calibrations (engine scale and misalignment, accelerometer scale, camera bias) are random walks on the ship's own clock: variance grows with ship time, use and damage. It enters Tier G as a per-arc deviation from the arrival value, the forecast as extra engine variance, and a new encounter as an aged prior.

**What 2C has to change.** Nothing already built to revision 1 is wrong; these are additions.

| Area | Change | Spec |
|---|---|---|
| Forecast hedge | split `L` into `Le` (inside validated spans) and `Ld`; two exponents with `sigma_e` and `sigma_h0` | §8.2, §8.3 |
| Extension record | store validated spans `{body, rMin, rMax, sigma_e, test}` with each adopted rule | §7.4, §11.1 |
| Tier G | per-arc deviation parameters for ship calibrations, prior σ from the arc's accrued aging; a parameter may be a sum of two | §3.7, §4.1 |
| Forecast engine term | current arc's value and covariance, plus aging since its last measurement | §3.7, §8.2 |
| New encounter | ship-calibration priors aged from the carrying arc | §3.7, §11.5 |

The six vectors 2C already works against are numerically unchanged (checked field by field; only `assumptionsHash` differs). `extension-validation` gained `earnedHedge.validatedRange` and one claim.

## What changed during the work, and why

Each was found by a failing check, not by argument.

Revision 2:

1. **"Per body, same body only" would have made the earned hedge inert.** Inside an encounter the held-out data are retained, so every validated distance is also a sampled one (hedge zero there), and a new body has no span. The spec therefore applies a shared-law rule's spans to every body. This is a reading of A5, flagged below.
2. **In a new encounter the new body dominates, not the hedge.** With 4 windows the local calibration was uncertain by 0.8% and swamped the earned hedge. The vector now tracks 24 h (8 windows); even so, the calibration term is the largest inside the validated span. That is A5's "an unknown carried knowledge does not cover", shown in numbers.
3. **The profile's inflation factor is noisy with few windows** (2.7 from four). Left as is because it errs wide and changing §5.4 would move numbers 2C already matches; listed as a limit (spec §13 item 14).
4. **Aging rates were first set too high for use**: 1% per √(100 m/s) loosened the scale by 0.5% for every small burn, more than a calibration can earn. Halved, with time at 0.2%/√day.

Revision 1:

1. **A global Newtonian fit over wide data does not converge** in the inverse-2.5 world (χ²/ν ≈ 270 000 after 60 iterations). That is the normal state before the player finds the law, so nothing may depend on it. The design became two tiers: local short-arc fits always converge.
2. **Fixed 2 h windows smeared radial misfit into fake tangential signal** (they span 165° near pericentre). Windows are now equal swept angle.
3. **A ridge-regularized profile collapsed to 1 between windows.** It now uses a smoothness penalty.
4. **The present state was biased.** A constant local scale on the uncalibrated law hid about 0.5 m/s of velocity error behind the 2 km range noise; the forecast was 5.9σ off after an hour. The anchor now fits on the calibrated law with a scale and a slope.
5. **One "scale the whole profile" uncertainty mode was too thin** (3.2σ inside sampled distances). The forecast now carries the full node covariance.
6. **A "rigid" forecast mode was dropped.** It kept the law's known misfit out of the mean and put it in the envelope; it was dishonest in direction (up to 50σ) and no crew would fly it.
7. **Burn-only confounding needed a real geometry.** A short radial burn gave ρ = 0.1–0.8. A thrust-supported hover, where only g − k_E u is observable, gives 0.993.

## Open questions and uncertainty

1. **Owner / integrator: which bodies a validated span applies to.** The request said "distance span per body". The spec records spans per body and, for a rule shared by all bodies (the exponent), applies them to every body, in this and later encounters (A4: "adopted rules apply everywhere"). The strict alternative, same body only, is one setting (`validatedRange.transfer`), but then the earned hedge never acts anywhere and a carried rule improves only the forecast's center line, not its envelope. Decide which is meant; the vector `carried-rule-forecast` shows the shared-law behaviour.
2. **Owner, playtest / 3E harness.** Aging rates, hedge σ_h = 0.3 and horizon fraction 0.1 are play values. The A5 check (fly with carried knowledge and no new measurements; it should not succeed comfortably) is where to tune them.
3. **Integrator: the ship's clock between encounters.** Aging needs ship time, commanded Δv and condition drops from the carrying attempt's last measurement to the next arrival (spec §3.7 item 3). Where transit time and wear between encounters are recorded is not defined yet.
4. **Owner / 3B / 1B.** Do accelerometers feel the Inverse Well's reversal? Recommended: no.
5. **Not vector-tested:** unseen-body mode, region thrust response, powered-window fits and per-burn response, asteroid probes, outlier flags, sigma points, pointing terms, a deviation split at a mid-arc damage event, camera-bias and accelerometer aging in a fit (their arithmetic is tested).
6. **Scale dependence.** Arc durations and cadences in the scenarios suit this scale (17 h orbit at r₀). A different scale may need scenario edits, not only `assumptions.json`.

Settled since the first handoff: P5 (approved, A4); recurrence resets the encounter world (P8); no outside review; no regeneration with 1C's sensor noise.

It needs condition changes as timestamped records in the attempt (an event with equipment id and the new condition is enough), and the carry record of open question 3.
4. **Integrator, `docs/DECISIONS.md`.** Under A5 item 5, record the reading chosen for open question 1.
5. **3E.** The harness check for A5 can read the forecast's horizon and the freshness split directly; no new interface is needed beyond requests 1–3.
