## What changed during the work, and why

Recorded because each was found by a failing check, not by argument.

1. **A global Newtonian fit over wide data does not converge** in the inverse-2.5 world (χ²/ν ≈ 270 000 after 60 iterations). That is the normal state before the player finds the law, so nothing may depend on it. The design became two tiers: local short-arc fits always converge.
2. **Fixed 2 h windows smeared radial misfit into fake tangential signal** (they span 165° near pericentre). Windows are now equal swept angle.
3. **A ridge-regularized profile collapsed to 1 between windows.** It now uses a smoothness penalty.
4. **The present state was biased.** A constant local scale on the uncalibrated law hid about 0.5 m/s of velocity error behind the 2 km range noise; the forecast was 5.9σ off after an hour. The anchor now fits on the calibrated law with a scale and a slope.
5. **One "scale the whole profile" uncertainty mode was too thin** (3.2σ inside sampled distances). The forecast now carries the full node covariance.
6. **A "rigid" forecast mode was dropped.** It kept the law's known misfit out of the mean and put it in the envelope; it was dishonest in direction (up to 50σ) and no crew would fly it.
7. **Burn-only confounding needed a real geometry.** A short radial burn gave ρ = 0.1–0.8. A thrust-supported hover, where only g − k_E u is observable, gives 0.993.

## Open questions and uncertainty

- **Owner, P5.** The model calibrates gravity against distance on its own (a table of numbers for sampled distances; it cannot extrapolate), while rules stay with the player. This fits P5 as proposed and makes familiar places forecastable before any rule is found, but it is a choice (spec §13 item 1; §7.6 covers "the model also learns rules").
- **Owner, playtest.** Hedge σ_h = 0.3 and horizon fraction 0.1 set how cautious the forecast looks outside sampled distances.
- **Integrator / 2A.** Do bodies and asteroids reset with the ship at recurrence? The estimator works either way.
- **Owner / 3B / 1B.** Do accelerometers feel the Inverse Well's reversal? Recommended: no.
- **Independence.** The plan has one party write the mathematics and the other implement it. This thread ran on Claude, as 2C will. Partial mitigations: the reference uses finite differences and shares no derivative code with a variational implementation; the propagator matches 1B's (GPT/Codex) vectors; every claim is machine-checked. An outside read of spec §5 and §8 would still be worth having.
- **Not vector-tested:** unseen-body mode, region thrust response, powered-window fits and per-burn response, asteroid probes, outlier flags, sigma points, pointing terms (spec §13 item 5).
- **Scale dependence.** Arc durations and cadences in the scenarios were chosen for 1B's candidate scale (17 h orbit at r₀). A different scale may need scenario edits, not only `assumptions.json`.
