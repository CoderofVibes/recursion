- P7: accelerometers sense only non-gravitational acceleration. P11: the ship never reads belief; belief-relative attitude targets are still rejected (the game layer resolves them).

## What to build
1. **Geometry in the data file** (`data/ship-baseline.json`): give every assembly, tank and appendage a 3D position `[x, y, z]`. Take z from `prototypes/room/ship-parts.js` where 1E set it (the `anchor` of each item with an `eq` id; the frame there is the same body frame), so the ship view and the physics agree; choose the rest sensibly ([ASSUMED], listed in the docs). Keep every x and y as it is. Describe the hull as prisms: the pressure hull (the stowed footprint's outline with its real height: a 5 m diameter body, so z from −2.5 to 2.5) and each appendage (radiator and solar wings) as its own thin prism that grows with deployment, in place of the old single conservative convex outline; say in the docs that the envelope is now the union of the parts and why that is acceptable for clearance (the objects are spheres hundreds of metres across or more). The four configurations keep their ids and meaning. Update `validate_baseline.py` and re-run it.
2. **Mass properties:** the full inertia tensor about the centre of mass by the parallel-axis theorem (`sym3.pointInertia`, `sym3.principalAxes`), returned as `Inertia { moments, axes }`. Each item needs an intrinsic inertia about three axes: keep the baseline's existing number as the yaw (zz) term so the yaw inertia is unchanged, and add roll and pitch terms from a stated simple shape (the pressure hull as a cylinder, wings as thin plates that grow with deployment, tanks and boxes as spheres or isotropic) in the data file, [ASSUMED] and listed. Report the principal inertia for each of the four configurations, full and empty tanks.
3. **Thrust and attitude:**
   - RCS: jets with 3D force directions on the four pods so the ship has torque about all three body axes and translation along all three, preferably as couples (no net force when turning, no net torque when translating, as far as the pod layout allows). 1E's drawing gives each pod nozzles fore, aft, up, down and outboard; use it as the layout unless the numbers say otherwise, and keep the existing jets' behaviour (the existing `jet-0` / `jet-1` / `bank-a` / `bank-b` modes, manual pod burns, the two-jets-per-pod limit, minimum impulse, warm-up, feeds, heat). Report the control authority: torque and angular acceleration per axis, force and acceleration per axis, per bank, and the cross-coupling.
   - A jet allocator: from a demanded body-frame force and torque to jet firings, respecting the per-pod limit and minimum impulse bits; deterministic.
   - Main engine: a two-axis gimbal that aims the thrust through the centre of mass in both y and z within the existing limit and rate, and two mount misalignment angles: the existing one about body +z (`values.misalignment`, parameter `thruster.<id>.misalignment`) and a new one about body −y that tilts the thrust toward +z (`values.misalignmentPitch`, parameter `thruster.<id>.misalignment-pitch`, default 0 unless the baseline states one).
   - Attitude control on three axes for `{reference: 'inertial', attitude}` (quaternion error) and `{reference: 'rate', rate}` (body-frame vector), with the existing gains as the starting point, **an attitude and a rate deadband and a slew-rate limit** (2F's request 3: today holding an attitude costs about 12 kg/h of RCS with nothing disturbing the ship, and a 180° turn peaks at 0.18 rad/s, 18 times the star trackers' limit of 0.01 rad/s). Choose values so that a quiet hold costs little, a turn stays within the star trackers' rate limit, and a 180° turn completes in a stated time; report RCS use for a hold (kg/h), for a 90° and a 180° turn about yaw and about pitch, and the peak rates. These are [ASSUMED] play values in named constants or the data file.
4. **Sensing and power:**
   - Camera: a 3D boresight and field of view (a cone); pointing at an object or along a direction in 3D; bearings as `[azimuth, elevation]` with an isotropic pointing error (σ_az = σ / cos(elevation), σ_el = σ; guard the poles); smear from the apparent angular rate in 3D; visibility flags as now.
   - Star trackers: attitude fixes as the rotation vector of the measured attitude (true attitude times a small error about each body axis), sigma per axis; the rate gate uses the magnitude of the angular velocity.
   - IMU: three-axis accelerometer (body frame, with a z bias) and three-axis gyro (with biases); the baseline's lever-arm policy as it stands.
   - Sunlight (A3): solar output and absorbed heat from the 3D incidence between `env.sunlight.direction` and the array normal in the inertial frame. Today's planar formula is cos(sun azimuth − yaw), i.e. the arrays face along body +x; keep that normal unless 1C's text says otherwise, so in-plane cases give the same numbers, and document it.
5. **Hands-on flying** (the `fly` command; replace the stub): `translate` drives the RCS along the three body axes; `yaw` and `pitch` command body rates about +z and −y (positive pitch raises the nose) up to a stated maximum rate, with **roll rate held at zero**; `throttle` drives the main engine (its pulse logic already quantises thrust); a neutral input stops thrust, nulls the rates and then **holds the attitude the ship has when the rates are nulled** (so released controls give a steady ship; this is what the coasting forecast assumes). Keep the lift stub's decisions that still make sense (a hands-on burn does not cancel a timed burn commanded since; bad input rejected; an unavailable engine is not woken). Report what a player can do: acceleration per axis, turn rates, RCS use per minute of translation and per turn.
6. **Speed** (2F's request 4): a tick costs about 0.23 ms and caps time warp near 2,000×; mass properties and geometry are recomputed several times a step. If caching them within a step (or across steps while nothing they depend on changes) is simple and keeps the step pure and bit-identical, do it and report before and after. Do not let 3D make it slower than it was.
7. **Tests:** keep the existing ones; add tests for each item above (allocator couples and authority per axis; the gimbal through an off-axis centre of mass; three-axis control converging with the deadband and slew limit, its propellant use; the hands-on behaviours; 3D sensing including the separate streams and the bit-identical planar components; 3D sun incidence; mass properties per configuration; hull prisms per configuration and during a transition).
8. **Docs:** `src/ship/README.md` rewritten for 3D (no "3D lift" section left); `docs/engineering/ship-baseline.md` and `gameplay-couplings.md` updated where the geometry, RCS layout, inertia, control or sensing changed, with every new value labelled ([ASSUMED] with its reasoning, or [ESTABLISHED: id] from `References/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md`).
