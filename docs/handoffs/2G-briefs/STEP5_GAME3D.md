## What to build
1. **Navigator** (`belief/standin-model.ts`) in 3D: the filter tracks position and velocity in three dimensions plus the local gravity scale (seven states) from azimuth, elevation and range fixes (using the reported per-component sigmas; mind the azimuth wrap and the 1/cos(elevation) factor); attitude from the star trackers' rotation-vector fixes as a quaternion and body rates from the gyros; thrust from executed `burn` and hands-on `fly` commands (main-engine throttle along the believed attitude; today the navigator ignores `fly`, so the believed ship lags while flying by hand); believed bodies and asteroids moving in 3D under the law in force; its stand-in forecast in 3D with a real `Cov3` per sample and the P10 horizon measured with the largest one-sigma axis; closest approaches with the hull's true reach (`hullReach`). For planar inputs it must give what it gives today to within rounding (state it).
2. **Environment** (`environment.ts`): sunlight with **cylindrical shadows** behind each body along the sun's 3D direction (A3); camera visibility in 3D: in shadow, the night side of a body, within the sun exclusion angle (a cone about the sun's direction), behind a body's sphere. Keep `fieldSignals['ship-environment']` as the ship expects it.
3. **Objectives** (`objectives.ts`, `encounter.ts`): distances in 3D; `orbit-band` counts revolutions by the angle swept about the body in 3D.
