## What a smaller version would lose

A single sheet or centre-point exposure would lose the layered navigation problem
and the danger to a deployed radiator after the cabin clears. A fixed hit counter
would lose glancing damage, selective failures and survivable damaged continuation.
A complete luminous mesh would give away unsensed structure and destroy the earned
reveal. Omitting coverage/covariance would turn silence into a false safe hole.
Removing progressive fragment growth, depth, uncertainty and the deployed ship would
lose the scale and tension of the first resolution on FLIGHT.

For A24, omitting response, noise or saturation would turn a local instrument into
an instantaneous exact meter. Omitting stale-data and ordering rules would let an
outage imply safety or an old record re-trigger a cleared alarm. Substituting the
worst component dose for the detector point would disclose radiation the monitor
never encountered.

The current delivery leaves integration dependencies visible instead of implementing
a second Black Box, damage engine or game loop in 3C's folders.
