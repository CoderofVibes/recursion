## Numerical and evidence limits

`sweepLatticeExposure` reports both an adaptive Simpson `dose` estimate and rigorous
`doseLower`/`doseUpper`. `resolved` is not a claim that the rigorous enclosure is as
narrow as the requested dose tolerance; `doseCertified` and `doseErrorBound` make
that separate. Refine ambiguous threshold events against the bounds. A caller must
supply a valid bound on every material point's speed and actual intermediate poses,
including deployment, COM changes and faults. Endpoint chords do not suffice.

Compact assemblies use S17's declared baseline mount-point approximation; radiator
and array vulnerabilities occupy their real prisms. Baseline collision geometry
still omits S17's proposed camera/antenna/engine guards: 3A must reconcile the shared
physical hull before claiming all exterior hardware clearance.

The angular channel cannot certify an empty 3D opening. That requires 2C's fitted
geometry, joint pose covariance, association/held-out checks and an independently
qualified detection certificate. The renderer defaults to unresolved. A snapshot
at `[t,t]` does not claim integrated camera history between ticks.

Informational Node contact probes of 42 assemblies and 96 strands over a 0.1 s tick
were about 9–20 ms after optimization, with an approximately 30 ms outlier; clear
queries were about 1 ms. This is not the A18 modest-laptop release measurement.
Full production contact chronology, browser frame budget and 3E seeded traversal
rates remain unverified. The exterior's sampled point route exceeds 3.3 km; neither
its complete resource cost nor an unavoidable two-lesson gate is demonstrated.

## What a smaller version would lose

A single sheet or centre-point exposure would lose the layered navigation problem
and the danger to a deployed radiator after the cabin clears. A fixed hit counter
would lose glancing damage, selective failures and survivable damaged continuation.
A complete luminous mesh would give away unsensed structure and destroy the earned
reveal. Omitting coverage/covariance would turn silence into a false safe hole.
Removing progressive fragment growth, depth, uncertainty and the deployed ship would
lose the scale and tension of the first resolution on FLIGHT.

The current delivery leaves integration dependencies visible instead of implementing
a second Black Box, damage engine or game loop in 3C's folders.
