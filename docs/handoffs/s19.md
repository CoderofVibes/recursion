## Claims, labelled

- **[FICTIONAL: P4; DEMONSTRATED: reference]** The unamplified tidal torque is `3.5 GM sqrt(r0)/r^3.5 * (n cross I n)`, summed over sources. **[ESTABLISHED: NASA SP-8024; Hallock et al., Eq. 3.10]** Its Newtonian limit is ordinary gravity-gradient torque. Sources: [NASA SP-8024](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19700014113), [ACS Without an Attitude](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20180004285/downloads/20180004285.pdf).
- **[DEMONSTRATED: reference]** The actual arrival radiators ship has a maximum **0.09660 N·m at Alpha 400 km**. 2G's **0.21 N·m** used a different reference inertia; do not label that result a physics error.
- **[ASSUMED: S4 exit-reference interpretation; DEMONSTRATED: source inspection and reference bounds]** Built C02 departure is **9,000 km from the barycentre**, not 9,000 km from Beta. S4's 1.05–1.30 exit-radius pocket therefore starts at **9,450 km**. Its radiators two-body torque bound is **3.656 µN·m**, at most **0.332 arcsec in 300 s** from rest; every swept load/configuration is below **0.413 arcsec** there. Healthy tracker noise is **8.25 arcsec per sample**. At 1 Hz, even the most optimistic configuration has less than **0.066σ** curvature significance over 300 s.
- **[DEMONSTRATED: reference; ASSUMED: ideal control delivery]** Close-body effects are real: the Alpha 400 km flyby turns **0.69464°** freely in 300 s. Suppressing only roll leaves **0.69473°**. Normal full attitude hold limits the ideal-delivery peak to **0.31386°**. The demand law is copied mathematically; the actual allocator/pulses are not simulated. At the outer pocket no hold demand occurs from rest, so omitted pulses cannot create the missing signal.
- **[DEMONSTRATED: reference]** Deployment does not magnify the chosen yaw response: Alpha 400 km gives **0.80884° stowed, 0.69464° radiators, 0.53476° all deployed**. These are isolated-body example flybys, not approved routes.
- **[ASSUMED: recommendation]** **Defer A02 in its current post-exit location.** A measurable close-pass instrument experiment is a possible later scope. Do not amplify tides, change fixed bodies, weaken controls, release roll, or relocate the ship for free.
