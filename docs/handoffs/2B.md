## Open questions and uncertainty

- Architecture/rating approval and route playability remain owner/integrator gate decisions. Nothing here promotes the 1C proposal into qualified hardware or a proven encounter.
- One environment snapshot allows at most one recorded packet per tick. 50 Hz requires `dt <= 0.02 s`; normal 10 Hz requires `dt <= 0.1 s`. Sampling gaps are retained at larger dt instead of synthesizing intermediate truth.
- Thermal equations are intentionally a stub: no freeze rupture, permanent heat damage, radiation damage, loop faults, crew consumable hazard, raw-frame acquisition or actual communications delivery. Ion thrust and two simultaneous directions in a single RCS pod are deferred.
- Fuel-cell ramp uses integrated available capacity then load-following curtailment, not a detailed stack controller. Local `faultPower` is a protection-test proxy, not a radiation model.
