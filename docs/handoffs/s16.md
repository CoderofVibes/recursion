## Claims, labelled

- [FICTIONAL: A15, S5] COM radial delivered propulsion reverses inside 40 km, transitions smoothly to ordinary at 50 km, leaves both tangential components and nominal torque unchanged, and consumes normal propellant. Cores are excluded at 1 km; fields do not overlap.
- [ASSUMED] Three nominal centres in garden kilometres: `(0,0,0)`, `(130,25,20)`, `(260,-25,-20)`; committed arrival `(-50.1,0,0) km` at `(35,0,0) m/s`; outgoing disc at x=330 km, radius 130 km. Empty local gravity-source list. No change to P4.
- [DEMONSTRATED: `vectors.json.unavoidability`] With even all forty jets and main/ion thrust, instantaneous pointing and dry mass, the entire reachable set is inside W1 by **26.250 s**. Maximum radius is **49,640.628 m**. Every admissible route from that state has encountered an influence, regardless of fuel. This is an all-control finite-thrust proof, not a sampled route claim.
- [DEMONSTRATED: `vectors.json.finite_outside_bypass`] **The stronger upstream fuel-wall requirement fails.** From the exterior skirt at x=−100 km, a finite-thrust route avoids all three influences for **726.769 kg main**, 76 m/s integrated impulse and 219.789 h. Turning intervals are allowed; RCS/power operation remains an integration check. The 6,000 kg load is plainly not a geometric barrier by itself.
- [DEMONSTRATED: `vectors.json.informed_crossings`] Either sign of an 8 s z burn at 600 s exits after **10858.917 s**, consuming **61.183 kg main**, with **4.132 km** minimum whole-ship core clearance. Positive z encounters W1/W2; negative z W1/W3. No automatic planner or required rule-adoption flag.
- [DEMONSTRATED: `vectors.json.old_belief`] A mistaken 30 s retrograde burn produces **1,664.711 m** sampled forecast error at 650 s, **433.674 s** before uncorrected contact. A 240 s turn allowance and 16 s lateral burn recover with **1.509 km** core clearance; **351.802 kg** total main used. Actual acquired tracking/display visibility and quaternion control still need testing.
- [DEMONSTRATED: `seeds.json`] **103/128 rejected (80.46875%)**: 99 lack the required forced-entry certificate; four fail the two-route control neighborhood. Every accepted seed passes 18 informed controls, the warning and recovery. Worst informed clearance **2.016 km**, warning lead **377.278 s**, recovery clearance **812.383 m**. These are finite-catalogue mechanics results, not continuous-range or full-session guarantees.
- [ASSUMED] Start harness admission at 1,000 kg main / 30 kg RCS carried in, reserving 200 kg main and allocating 10 kg RCS to operation. Nominal informed flight leaves **738.817 kg main above that reserve**. No refill or state replacement is authorized.

## Open questions and uncertainty

1. **Owner: accept committed-arrival unavoidability while allowing upstream exterior detours?** Recommend yes for the prototype. A literal global fuel wall is not delivered by this layout; if required, additional physical containment/transfer design is necessary. Do not describe this choice as already approved.
2. **Owner: use the 25-entry catalogue initially?** Recommend yes, after actual ship/sensor admission; extend the ranges only with new certificates.
3. **Owner: preliminary 1,000 kg main / 30 kg RCS Garden budget?** Recommend as harness targets, then reconcile against the full expedition, including N03 damage and actual RCS costs.

The first-influence proof does not force a dramatic reversal or learning: an early
braking player can graze the weak outer band. Sensor FOV, fixed-sun visibility,
true delivered thrust, finite attitude/gimbal operation, electrical/thermal
endurance, new obstacles and arbitrary inherited-state combinations are not
certified by the Python reference. No seed should receive a release “fair” label
until those checks pass.
