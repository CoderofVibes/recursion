## Open questions and uncertainty

Owner decisions, one at a time, with recommendations in the study:

1. Harden the console? **Yes**, retain honest stale/upset presentation.
2. End the attempt on irrecoverable loss of both PDUs? **Yes**, not on a single
   trip, camera failure or survivable appendage damage.
3. Include a <=1 KiB uninterpreted channel tile in a 1 Hz ordinary diagnostic
   packet when sensing is active? **Yes**, so the loss can suggest structure;
   the current 0.05 Hz setting cannot guarantee useful observations in 11 s.
4. Start the eight-layer prototype at 1.4 m/s with a 125 kg local RCS test budget?
   **Yes provisionally**, contingent on actual allocator and later-route reserves.
5. Keep 8 GB and optional raw capture? **Yes**, make real power/fresh-data and
   remaining-capacity constraints visible instead of imposing a new tiny store.

The reference assumes feed capacity before PDU loss, compact equipment mounts,
specified outer guards, known feature associations, good recorded relative poses
and calibrated fictional detection coverage. Production must validate those. It
does not establish a blind-survival probability, a successful all-layer sensing
schedule, an affordable bypass, actual no-pause control or expedition reserves.
The sensors-only loss sequence does not itself prove a lattice; retained channel
tiles and a powered follow-up survey provide the structural evidence.
