# Ship gameplay couplings — 1C proposal

**Status: [ASSUMED] proposed implementation priorities, subject to owner approval and playtest.** Ratings and hardware inventories live in [the baseline](ship-baseline.md) and [its JSON](../../data/ship-baseline.json). This document adds no hardware or independent tuning constants. Flight is three-dimensional (DECISIONS A11); where a coupling below spoke of the plane or of yaw alone it now speaks of space and attitude, and the geometry, jets and control values 2G added are in the baseline's Three dimensions section. The bibliography IDs below resolve through [References/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md](../../References/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md).

The ordering is a proposed ranking by useful player decisions relative to implementation cost. Value and cost are qualitative engineering judgments, not measured results. **Low** cost means a coupling between existing state variables; **medium** adds state, allocation, or transition logic; **high** needs geometry, measurements, or several interacting subsystems. All phase assignments, costs, and verification targets are **[ASSUMED]**. Source labels establish the mechanism, not this ship's numerical ratings or its playability.

## Suggested verification targets

These are proposed acceptance checks, **[ASSUMED]**, not claims of passing tests or playable routes. Implement them in the owning phase's tests; preserve IDs so the eventual `src/ship/README.md` can trace implemented behavior back to this proposal.

| Couplings | Check and observable invariant |
|---|---|
| C01, C05, C14 | On a visible calibrated target, a supported higher-quality mode improves the declared measurement uncertainty or cadence. With the target occluded, no mode produces a valid optical fix. Coast through a nonzero gravity field: the simulated accelerometer still has only its bias/noise and any other explicitly modeled non-gravitational forces. |
| C02 | Drain each propellant reservoir separately and compare mass, centre of mass and inertia with direct sums. Apply a force through the current centre of mass and verify zero moment on all three axes; apply the same force through an offset mounting point and verify the correct sign and magnitude. Turn with couples (no net force) and translate with no net torque. No propellant reservoir goes negative. |
| C03, C13 | Account for every joule supplied, stored, converted, consumed and lost. Verify bus ratings and battery thermal/current limits independently of total generation. Exhaust supply: discretionary loads shed in the declared order; life support remains requested, with any unmet demand reported. Reactants and product water conserve mass under the baseline retention policy. |
| C04, C11, C12 | Move one appendage while holding the other fixed. Geometry, centre of mass and thermal capability follow that same intermediate state. Loss of a pump lowers heat transport even when the radiator surface is cold. Low heat load with excessive cooling must permit the declared freeze-risk state; extra radiator area is not always beneficial. |
| C06 | Increase sample rate and check the declared recorder power, heat and acquisition-capacity changes. A missed sample remains missing. Preserve P8's retained records, commands and issued forecasts according to the agreed archive policy; physical capacity must not silently delete historical evidence promised by P8. |
| C07, C09 | Replaying the same start state and commands produces the same component exposures and faults. Two paths with equal centre-point clearance but different appendage clearance produce appropriately different exposures. A protected latch-up can interrupt service without destroying every peer; a burned component remains failed after reset and after leaving the field. |
| C08 | Increasing reconstruction allocation changes only the fictional observation channel's declared quality/cadence while consuming real electrical/thermal resources. No powered mode adds physical equipment, grants unexplained immunity, or provides hidden truth to the ordinary forecaster. |
| C10 | A cold standby branch does not produce nominal thrust before its declared readiness criteria are met. Switching preserves already consumed propellant and damage; the new branch's geometry and force directions determine its actual control authority. |
| C12, C15 | Change attitude (yaw and pitch) with fixed environmental inputs. Solar output, observation eligibility and link availability respond through their respective geometry. With no receiver available, transmission consumes the declared resources but reports no delivered data. |
| C16 | Use the declared NEXT operating point: verify electrical draw, propellant flow, exported propulsion energy and onboard heat without double counting. Over a representative expedition interval, report actual velocity change before choosing to include the mode. |
| C17 | A checking path consumes its declared resources and can report disagreement. It must not inspect truth or claim correctness solely because multiple implementations agree. |

## Implementation boundary and sequencing

**[ASSUMED] Phase 2 minimum:** implement C01–C06 and the basic state needed for C09–C10, C12–C13 and C15. Thermal nodes can remain simple, but energy, mass, pointing and measurement semantics must already be consistent. A configuration change should have one authoritative state used by all consumers. Keep actual equipment availability separate from the player's estimate of it.

**[ASSUMED] Phase 3 minimum:** deepen C04 and C09–C13, add C07–C08, and complete C14 where observation quality matters. Prioritize understandable procedures: inspect the measurements, reserve power, make a configuration change, operate, then compare the recorded consequences. C16–C17 are optional until a playtest establishes enough value to justify their interfaces.

**[ASSUMED] Shared-interface requests:** expose actual actuator vectors and mounting positions to the simulation; publish timestamped sensor measurements and validity to the model; publish configuration-dependent geometry to collision/exposure consumers; publish per-component lattice exposure to condition logic. Resolve these through the integrator's contracts. This document does not define orbital dynamics, encounter routes, or model-learning rules.

**[ASSUMED, brief §9] State carries through an attempt:** no coupling automatically repairs equipment, replenishes propellant/reactants, restores battery charge, or clears heat at an encounter boundary. A source charging a battery is an explicit energy transfer. A power cycle restores only recoverable logic/service state. **[FICTIONAL, P8]** Recurrence and evidence persistence are explicit separate rules.
