# Art bible: the view through the window

For the threads that build the window's visuals: 2D (window and sky), 3B (funnels), OP (opening and Earth), 4D (openings), 3C (radiation clouds). It describes the chosen look in words precise enough to build procedurally. The reference images are not in the repository (A23); their job IDs are in [selections.md](selections.md). The prompts are in [window-prompts.md](window-prompts.md).

**Status:** the general rules below hold now. The per-scene looks are filled in as the owner chooses images.

## General rules

1. **Real space first.** True-colour astrophotography: pinpoint stars, black vacuum, high dynamic range, no lens flare, no diffraction spikes (the window is an eye, not a telescope), no light beams in vacuum. Light shows only where it falls on a surface (P02).
2. **Violet means fiction.** Night Watch's `well`/`lattice` violet (`#9b8cff`, cores toward `#ece6ff`) is for the funnels, the radiation clouds and the openings' rims only. Nothing natural is violet.
3. **The hologram must read on top.** The flight hologram is drawn in front of the window. Keep the sky dim and low in saturation, and keep it clear of the instrument hues: forecast teal, thrust magenta, velocity lime, Black Box orange, caution yellow and warning red. Nebulae stay faint and small.
4. **Warm room, cold outside.** The room is warm near-black (Night Watch). The outside is neutral to cold, so the window reads as a different world.
5. **Show only what is true.** Nothing the physics contradicts: no big nearby planet that isn't in the data, no lattice (A16), no visible effect of a well beyond its shape and light, no accretion disc. Bodies appear at their data's sizes.
6. **Subtly wrong.** This universe's sky is not ours: no single Milky Way band, star colours that lean to deep red and blue-white. Our real sky appears only at Earth (opening, ending, and through the openings).
